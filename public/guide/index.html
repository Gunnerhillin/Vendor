Your first 15 minutes

Look at the navigation, product collections and featured brand. This is what shoppers see. Empty sections mean approved inventory still needs to be added.

Browse freely. Do not place a real order while launch testing is unfinished.

Open homepage ↗

Share this page with a brand you would like to recruit. It explains the marketplace and links to the application. Ask for product photos, descriptions, prices including shipping, available quantities and processing times.

Your first goal: help 3–5 brands apply. Do not promise an unconfirmed commission or payment date.

Open brand invitation ↗

The brand creates its own account and completes the application. Each brand should use an email address it controls. Applying does not automatically approve the brand.

Application link to share: https://www.cutievalley.com/vendor/apply

Open brand application ↗

Create and verify your own account. Send Gunner the email you registered with. He can find your account under Admin → Users & Roles and assign admin access if you will review brands and products. Then sign in again.

Admin access allows changes across the store. Outreach alone does not require admin access. Never share a password.

Customer tour

From discovering a brand to receiving an order

Search for a product, open a category from the homepage, or visit a brand’s shop by clicking its name. Only approved listings from approved brands should appear.

Check photos, descriptions and prices as a shopper would.

Open product search ↗

Open any real product. Review its photos, description, price, available options and brand. Select the correct option and quantity before adding it to your cart.

If no products are available, onboarding and approval come first.

Find a product ↗

Review the products, quantities and brands. The launch plan includes shipping in product prices; separate brands prepare separate packages.

The newer secure checkout flow is built on the development branch and is awaiting deployment and Stripe testing.

Open cart ↗

In the new flow, Continue to secure payment opens Stripe. Enter a US shipping address and review the final total and tax before paying. Confirmation appears only after payment is verified.

Not cleared for real purchases yet. Only run payment tests with the test setup confirmed by Gunner.

Open checkout ↗

Sign in to the account used for the purchase. Open Orders to see purchased items, fulfillment status and tracking when the vendor adds it. Brands may ship at different times.

A checkout redirect alone is not proof of a successful payment.

Open my orders ↗

Find the delivered item and use the return request control. Explain the issue clearly. A vendor reviews the request, and an admin can override a decision. Follow the approved return instructions.

The return window still needs final confirmation. Return approval, physical receipt and an actual refund are separate steps.

Use Contact for questions. About explains the business; the footer links to privacy, terms and refund information.

Company details, policy wording and actual support delivery still need final launch verification.

Vendor tour

Help a brand go from application to its first shipment

Submit the brand information and requested samples. An admin reviews the application. If more information is requested, provide it before trying to publish products.

Applying does not mean the brand or its products are live.

Open application ↗

After approval, sign in as the brand and open Get Started. Complete the brand profile, payment onboarding and first listing. Use this checklist when walking a new vendor through setup.

You need that brand’s own account to view its setup. Do not use your partner/admin account as the brand.

Open vendor checklist ↗

Add the brand name, description, logo and banner. Set an honest processing time and explain how orders will ship. Include shipping costs in product prices.

First-launch workflow: buy labels with the brand’s carrier or shipping service, then enter tracking manually. Automatic shipping integration is not ready.

Open vendor settings ↗

Follow the payment onboarding link from the vendor checklist. The brand owner supplies the business and bank details directly to Stripe. Return to CutieValley and check the setup status.

Never send bank details or identity documents to your outreach partner. Commission terms still need confirmation.

Open payment setup checklist ↗

Click Add Product. Enter the title, description, category, price including shipping, available stock and clear photos. Click Save Draft if it is unfinished.

A draft is private. Check spelling, photo quality and stock before submitting.

Open vendor products ↗

Click Submit for Review. Wait for admin approval. If rejected, read the review note, correct the listing and submit again. An approved listing can appear in the shop.

Editing a published listing’s content sends it back for review. Stock-only changes use the stock update control.

Open listings ↗

In the new fulfillment flow, open a paid order, read the complete delivery address and buy the label. Click Ship Order, select the carrier, enter the tracking number and confirm the shipment.

This improved workflow is built but not deployed yet. Shipping a single brand’s items does not mean the entire multi-brand order has shipped.

Open vendor orders ↗

Review return requests and explain approval or denial clearly. Use Earnings to review recorded amounts. Admin verifies delivery before reviewing payment to the brand.

Recorded earnings are not proof that money reached a bank. Actual transfers and refunds are still being completed and tested.

Admin tour

Review the marketplace without guessing where to click

Sign in with your own admin account. On a smaller screen, use the navigation menu to choose the section you need. If you return to the homepage, your account may not have admin access yet.

The dashboard checklist is a reminder, not proof that an integration is working.

Open admin dashboard ↗

Open Vendors, choose a pending application and inspect its business details and samples. Click Approve when ready, or Reject with a clear explanation of what needs fixing.

Approving the brand enables its setup; it does not approve all products automatically.

Open vendor review ↗

Open Products and choose pending listings. Inspect the photos, title, description, category, price and stock. Approve a complete listing or reject it with useful feedback.

Confirm the brand is approved first. Open the storefront afterward to verify the listing appears.

Open product review ↗

Under Featured brand, choose an approved brand for the homepage spotlight. Launch setup also contains the application link you can copy for outreach.

Choose a brand with a complete profile and approved products. Check the homepage after changing it.

Open launch setup ↗

Open Orders and inspect each brand’s items and tracking. In the new workflow, check the carrier’s delivery evidence before clicking Confirm verified delivery.

Delivery verification does not send money. These improved controls are awaiting deployment.

Open admin orders ↗

Read the customer’s reason and the vendor’s response. Review or override the decision with a clear note. Record the condition of items actually received before financial reconciliation.

Do not treat an approved request or a completed label as an issued Stripe refund. Refund execution is still unfinished.

Open admin returns ↗

Review recorded earnings and any reconciliation holds. The agreed launch policy is admin review after verified delivery. Actual money movement must be confirmed separately.

The current screen is accounting only. Transfers, refund interlocks and transfer reversals are still being built.

Open payout overview ↗

Find a teammate by their registered email. Assign admin only if they need store-wide review and management access. Have them sign in again after a change.

Do not change customer or vendor roles just to troubleshoot a page.

Open users and roles ↗

Before inviting shoppers, confirm real inventory, company/support information, return terms, commission, payment testing, order emails, refunds and vendor payments.

Flash deals and coupons are not cleared for launch. Do not promise discounts until server pricing and timing are verified.

If you get stuck

I can’t open an admin page.

Sign in with your own account. If it takes you back to the homepage, ask Gunner to check your role under Users & Roles. This guide is public, but management pages require authorization.

A product isn’t appearing in the shop.

Check the brand’s approval, then the product’s approval. A draft or pending listing is not public. Editing published content can send it back to review. Check stock and refresh the storefront.

Add Product is unavailable.

The vendor must be approved. Ask the admin to review the application; do not switch account roles as a workaround.

The guide mentions a button I can’t see.

Check whether the step is labeled as awaiting deployment. You may also be using the wrong type of account. Send Gunner the page address, the action you tried and the message shown. Leave passwords, bank details and customer addresses out of screenshots.

What should I tell a brand right now?

Applications are welcome while launch testing continues. Ask them to prepare a complete profile and their first products. Shipping must be included in prices. Admin will review vendor payments after delivery. Confirm commission and return terms with Gunner before making commitments.

Small glossary

Vendor: a brand selling on the site. Draft: a private, unfinished listing. Pending: waiting for review. Tracking: the carrier’s shipment number. Refund: money returned to the customer. Vendor transfer: money moved to a brand’s Stripe account; its bank payout is a separate step.

This guide contains no account data. Sharing it does not grant admin access.
