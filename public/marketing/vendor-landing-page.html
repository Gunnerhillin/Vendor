How It Works

Three Steps to Start Selling

From application to your first sale, we've made it as simple as possible.

01

Apply

Fill out a quick application with your brand details, product samples, and business info. Our team reviews each application before approving a brand.

02

Set Up Your Shop

After approval, add photos, descriptions, prices, and stock. Save drafts until you are ready to submit your listings for review.

03

Get Ready to Sell

Approved listings can appear in the marketplace. Complete payment onboarding and confirm your seller terms before accepting orders.
